Meet Your Coach

Priscilla Claude

Army officerMomCertified Personal TrainerCertified NutritionistFounder, Reform Athletics

I am an Army officer and a mom, and I coach for a living. Most days those three jobs overlap, so I know what a full calendar feels like. I know how easily your own training slides to the bottom of the list.

That is why I built Reform Athletics the way I did. I come to your home, meet you at the gym, or coach you online, and the plan is built around the schedule you actually have, not the one a template assumes.

Many of the women I work with are in their 30s and 40s, carrying a lot, and noticing their body respond differently than it used to. They do not need a louder workout. They need structure, a coach who pays attention, and a standard they can keep.

You fall to your standard. So we raise it, one realistic choice at a time.
