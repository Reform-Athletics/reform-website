The Reform Process

Your plan is ready before you start.

1

Consultation & Assessment

We meet virtually to talk goals, schedule and history. When it helps, I assess how you move in person, at your home or the gym.

2

Your Custom Program

I build it and send it before you begin, so you can review it and plan your groceries.

3

Get-Started Check-In

We walk through the plan together and adjust on the spot. This is separate from your monthly check-ins.

4

First Session

You train, with the plan already in your hands.

5

30-Day Progress Review

We look at what is working, then refine from there.
