6. Your rights

You can cancel this anytime

You may revoke this authorization at any time by giving written notice to each provider listed and to Reform Athletics at priscilla@reformathletics.net or 6688 Nolensville Rd, Ste 108 #2214, Brentwood, TN 37027. Revoking stops future sharing. It does not undo anything already shared while this authorization was in effect.

Signing is your choice

Your providers cannot make your treatment, payment, insurance enrollment or eligibility for benefits depend on whether you sign this form.

How your information is protected once shared

Reform Athletics is a fitness and nutrition coaching business, not a health care provider or health plan. Once your information is shared with Reform Athletics, federal privacy law (HIPAA) may no longer protect it, and it could in principle be shared again. Reform Athletics commits to the following: your information is used only to plan and coach your training and nutrition; it is never sold or used for marketing; it is shared only with the providers you list here, unless the law requires otherwise or in an emergency; it is stored in private, access-controlled systems; and it is deleted 12 months after you stop training, unless you ask for it to be deleted sooner.
