This Client Service Agreement ("Agreement") is between Reform Athletics LLC ("Reform Athletics," "Coach") and the client ("Client"), effective the start date below.

1. Services

Reform Athletics will provide the selected service as described in the plan summary above. All programming is custom-built for Client; no templates are used.

2. Investment and payment

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Services begin once payment is received.

Client initials

3. Scheduling, cancellations and late arrivals

Sessions must be cancelled or rescheduled at least 24 hours in advance. Sessions cancelled with less notice, or missed without notice, are counted as completed and are not refunded or credited. If Client arrives late, the session still ends at its scheduled time; after 15 minutes without contact, the session is treated as a no-show. If Coach must cancel, the session will be rescheduled at no cost.

Client initials

Packages. Package sessions must be used within 45 days of the first session. Unused sessions expire and are non-refundable and non-transferable unless Coach agrees in writing.

Monthly plans. Monthly plans renew automatically each month on the billing date. Client may cancel at any time with at least 7 days' notice before the next billing date; service continues through the end of the paid month. Partial months are not refunded.

4. Nutrition guidance

Nutrition guidance from Reform Athletics is general wellness coaching. It is not medical nutrition therapy and is not a substitute for advice from a physician or registered dietitian. Client is responsible for disclosing all food allergies, intolerances and medical conditions, and for consulting a physician before making significant dietary or supplement changes.

Client initials

5. Client responsibilities

Client agrees to provide complete and accurate health information, to update Coach promptly about any change in health, injury, medication or pregnancy status, and to stop exercising and tell Coach immediately if they feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or discomfort. For in-home sessions, Client agrees to provide a safe, clear training space, to secure pets during sessions, and confirms that any equipment Client owns is in safe working condition.

6. Results

Results depend on many factors, including consistency, nutrition, sleep, stress, genetics and health history. Reform Athletics does not guarantee any specific result, including weight loss or body composition change.

7. Privacy

Reform Athletics keeps Client's personal and health information confidential and uses it only to design and deliver Client's program. It will not be sold or shared without Client's permission, except where required by law or in an emergency.

8. Ending this agreement

Either party may end this Agreement with written notice (email or text). Payments for sessions already delivered, expired package sessions, and the current paid month are non-refundable. Coach may end services immediately if Client's health or conduct makes training unsafe, in which case unused prepaid package sessions will be refunded on a pro-rated basis.

Founding client rate. As a founding client, Client's rate for this service is locked at for as long as Client's membership or package remains continuous. If Client's membership or package ends, Client may restart at this founding rate within 30 days of the end date. After 30 days, Reform Athletics' then-current rates apply.

9. General

This Agreement, together with the Liability Waiver and Release, is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing. It is governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee. If any part is found unenforceable, the rest remains in effect. Both parties agree that electronic signatures on this Agreement are legally binding.
