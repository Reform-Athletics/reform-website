Text Messaging (SMS) Program

Reform Athletics LLC · (615) 905-6358

Reform Athletics sends text messages only to people who ask for them. There are two ways to opt in. Consent to texts is never required to buy anything or book a session.

1. Online booking form

When you book at book.reformathletics.net, the form shows two separate, optional checkboxes. Neither is pre-checked and neither is required to book.

Appointment and coaching texts (non-marketing)

I agree to receive text messages from Reform Athletics about my appointments and coaching (reminders, scheduling and service updates). Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Offers and promotions (marketing, separate)

Yes, also send me occasional offers and promotions from Reform Athletics by text and email, including The Edit newsletter. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Promotional texts go only to people who checked the second box.

2. Texting us first (printed postcards)

Our direct-mail postcards include this call to action:

Text [CODE] to (615) 905-6358 for a free consult. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Privacy: reformathletics.net/privacy.html

Texting the code opts you in to replies about your inquiry, your consult and your appointments only. It does not opt you in to promotional texts.

What you'll receive

  • Replies to your messages and consult or session reminders
  • Coaching check-ins and program updates (clients)
  • Payment links for services you asked for
  • Occasional offers, only if you checked the marketing box

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out or get help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. Reply HELP for help, or email support@reformathletics.net. Reply START to resubscribe.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.
